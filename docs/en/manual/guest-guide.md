# Guest guide (one page)

> Hand this to whoever is going to take part. The same help is on the sign-in
> page itself: the "Trouble getting in?" link (`ayuda.html`).

1. **What you need.** A computer or phone with a camera and microphone; an
   up-to-date browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox; Safari on iPhone/iPad);
   headphones if you have them; good Wi-Fi or a cable. On a phone, keep it
   plugged in.
2. **Getting in.** Open the link (or scan the QR code), type your name and the
   PIN the production team gave you.
3. **If the browser says "Your connection is not private".** This happens when
   the link is an address made of numbers. If the production team sent it to
   you: *Advanced → Proceed*. If it came from someone you don't know, don't go
   ahead.
4. **Camera and microphone permission.** Tap *Allow*. If you blocked it: the
   padlock in the address bar → allow → reload. Without a camera you can still
   join with just the microphone.
5. **Waiting room.** The operator can see you, may talk to you privately, and
   lets you in. There's nothing else you need to do.
6. **Inside.** 🎙️ microphone, 📷 camera (red = off), share your screen, send a
   file, chat and raise your hand. If you're a speaker you'll see your turn and
   your clock.
7. **If something goes wrong.** Reload the page (you keep your place); if the
   internet dropped, it reconnects by itself; close Zoom/Meet/Teams if they're
   using the camera; try another browser.
8. **To look and sound good.** Light in front of you, camera at eye level, a
   quiet room.
