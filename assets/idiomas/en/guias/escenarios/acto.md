# Institutional ceremony or graduation

## What it's for
A ceremony with protocol: entrance of the flags, anthem, speeches by officials, handing out of
diplomas with names on screen, closing. For families in the hall and online.

## Questions of its own
- Which institution? Logo or coat of arms (of the school, the province, the country)?
- The order of the ceremony? Anthem (a file or live)? Who speaks?
- Are diplomas handed out? The list of names (spreadsheet)?
- A stream for the families? Screens in the hall? Music for the entrances?

## What to set up
- Blocks of the protocol with their cues: anthem (video or music), speeches with a lower third,
  the handing out.
- A lower third per graduate from the list (a graphic with the name: one cue per name or a list
  that advances).
- Coat of arms and logo as a DSK layer; title slates for each moment.
- Music for the entrances (playlist_crear) and the screens in the hall.

## Check
The anthem plays and can be seen; the names without typos (go over them with the operator); the
stream.

## Hand over
The ceremony's web page with the link to the stream; the announcer's teleprompter with the
protocol.
