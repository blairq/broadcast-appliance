# Religious ceremony or celebration

## What it's for
A celebration with fixed parts, music, song lyrics or readings on screen, and relatives watching
from far away.

## Questions of its own
- Which celebration and what parts does it have? Who leads it?
- Song lyrics or readings on screen? (Ask for the texts.)
- Live or recorded music? Screens at the venue?
- A stream for those who aren't there? Private (unlisted link)?

## What to set up
- Blocks for each moment; lyrics as slides or text graphics, one per verse.
- Scenes: “Altar/Stage”, “Wide shot”, “Lyrics” (the camera with the lyrics at the bottom).
- An unlisted stream if it is private.

## Check
The lyrics without mistakes and in order; the music's audio (without clipping).

## Hand over
The private link to the stream for the families.
