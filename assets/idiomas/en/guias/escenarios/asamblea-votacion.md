# Large assembly with voting and quorum

## What it's for
The assembly of an association, union, party or federation: many attendees who check in at the
door, quorum, several votes and results everyone has to see.

## Questions of its own
- How many have the right to vote? Is there a roll (spreadsheet)? Does everyone vote the same or
  is it weighted?
- How do they check in: the invitation's QR code at the door, by hand, by ID?
- How do they vote: with their phone (the audience page with its QR code), a counted show of
  hands, with the room's central unit?
- The agenda and the majority for each item? A quorum of how many?
- Big screens in the room for the results? A stream?

## What to set up
- A complete roll with `vota: true` and contact (email or WhatsApp) for the invitation with a QR
  code.
- Check-in at the door (padron_puerta / padron_acreditar) and a live quorum (quorum_presentes).
- The agenda with documents; a vote per item.
- Scenes: “Head table”, “Speaker”, “Vote in progress”, “Result”; a slate with the quorum.
- The room's screens with the vote (pantalla_*) and the QR code for voting in view.

## Check
A FIXED address before printing or sending the QR codes (acceso_remoto_probar_direccion); a test
vote from a phone; the results screen.

## Hand over
Invitations with a QR code to each one; the audience link for voting; at the end, the minutes with
the quorum and the results.
