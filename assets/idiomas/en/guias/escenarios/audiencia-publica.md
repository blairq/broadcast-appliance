# Public hearing

## What it's for
A hearing where the registered speakers give their opinion on a topic, in order and with a fixed
time, and everything is recorded for the file.

## Questions of its own
- The topic and the authority who chairs it? How many registered? Is there a list of speakers?
- Time per speaker? Can remote people speak? With documents?
- Are the documents of the file published? Is it transcribed?

## What to set up
- A roll with the registered speakers in order (role: speaker) and their contact.
- A speakers queue with a fixed time and a warning (speakers_config, banca_tiempo).
- A lower third with each speaker's name and organization; the speaker's clock on screen.
- Speech to text for the transcript; the file's documents on the web page.

## Check
Remote speakers tested; the speaker's clock visible; the transcript being saved.

## Hand over
The web page with the file; at the end, the minutes with each speaker and the time they used.
