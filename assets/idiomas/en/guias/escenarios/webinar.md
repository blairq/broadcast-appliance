# Webinar or training

## What it's for
A training for people who sign up and watch from home: presentation, demo,
questions and material to download.

## Questions of its own
- How many sign-ups are expected? What information do they sign up with (email, WhatsApp)?
- Who presents? With slides or an on-screen demo?
- Live questions, a poll, a certificate of attendance?
- Downloadable material?

## What to build
- Roll with the sign-ups and their contact; invitations with a link.
- Scenes: “Presenter”, “Screen” (window capture), “Presentation/PDF”, “Questions”.
- Downloadable documents on the event website.
- Audience questions, and votes used as a poll.

## Check
The event website opens and lets people download the material; test stream.

## Hand over
Invitations, the website with the material, and the recording afterwards.
