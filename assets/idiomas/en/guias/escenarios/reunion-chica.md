# Small meeting

## What it's for
A team meeting, a family one, a small committee: few people, sometimes someone remote.
What matters: everyone sees and hears each other, it gets recorded and nobody has to fight with
the technology.

## Questions of its own
- How many are there and how many join from outside? From which devices (phone, computer)?
- Is it recorded? Are minutes or a summary needed afterwards (speech to text)?
- Is there an agenda? Does anyone share their screen or documents?
- Is it streamed to anyone else or just among themselves? (Almost always: just among themselves.)

## What to build
- A short event with two or three blocks (welcome, topics, closing) and its agenda.
- “Head table” scene: the room camera with the remote people in a grid; a “Screen” scene for what
  they share; a PDF scene if there are documents.
- Roll with each remote person's contact to send them the link.
- Recording and, if they want minutes, speech to text turned on (texto_flujos).

## Check
Tunnel and room link from another device; the room audio (no echo: remote people
with headphones, invitado_prueba).

## Hand over
The room link for the remote people (invitation per person) and, when it ends, where the
recording is (evento_grabaciones) and the summary if one was asked for.
