# Panel or round table

## What it's for
A moderator leads several panelists who talk in turns, sometimes with questions from the
audience. The challenge: always show whoever is speaking and keep everyone within their time.

## Questions of its own
- How many panelists, who, in person or remote? Who moderates?
- Format: opening round, cross questions, closing? Times?
- Questions from the audience?

## What to build
- Blocks per round; a lower third for each panelist.
- Scenes: “Wide shot”, “Panelist” (one per person, or the camera that follows the microphone:
  seguir_microfono), “Head to head” (debate_config), “Remote grid”.
- Speaker queue with times (speakers_config).

## Check
Each remote panelist tested; that the camera switch follows whoever is speaking.

## Hand over
The panelists' links, the website, the QR code for questions.
