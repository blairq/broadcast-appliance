# Meeting of a condominium association, cooperative or club

## What it's for
The ordinary or extraordinary meeting of a building, cooperative or club: agenda, financial
statements, budgets, voting (sometimes by unit percentage), signed minutes.

## Questions of its own
- How many owners or members? Do they vote per person or by percentage (unit, shares)?
- In person and by video call? Who chairs and who takes the minutes?
- The agenda and documents (financial statements, budgets) to share beforehand?
- Special majorities? A second call at what time?

## What to set up
- A roll with each owner/member, their unit or voting weight and contact.
- The agenda with documents (financial statements as a PDF, budgets) on the event's web page.
- A PDF scene to show the financial statements live; a vote per item.
- An invitation (notice of meeting) with a link and a QR code, sent with the advance notice the
  bylaws require.

## Check
A stable address before sending the notice of meeting; a test remote attendee; the documents open.

## Hand over
The notices of meeting; the web page with the documents; at the end, the minutes with attendance,
quorum and votes.
