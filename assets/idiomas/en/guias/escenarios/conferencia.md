# Conference or talk with an audience

## What it's for
An open talk: speakers (some remote), an audience present and online that asks questions, and a
stream to a platform.

## Questions of its own
- Who speaks, in what order, for how long each? Any remote? Do they bring a presentation?
- Is there a moderator? Questions from the audience (from their phones, moderated)?
- Registration in advance? Limited seats? What information do they register with (email,
  WhatsApp)?
- Is it streamed? Where? Is it recorded?
- The organizer's logo, sponsors, standby music?

## What to set up
- Blocks per speaker with their lower third (real name and title).
- Scenes: “Speaker”, “Presentation” (slides/PDF), “Remote speaker”, “Questions”, “Standby”.
- Questions from the audience (publico_pagina, pregunta_moderar) and their QR code on screen.
- The stream configured (stream_destino_agregar or the platform) and the recording.
- A standby slate with the start time (evento_preparar) and music (playlist_crear).

## Check
Remote speakers tested; the questions QR code opening; a private test stream beforehand.

## Hand over
Links for the speakers, the event's web page and the stream link; the questions QR code.
