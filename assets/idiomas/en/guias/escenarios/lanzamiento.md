# Presentation or launch

## What it's for
An event to show something new: opening video, a polished presentation, guests, press questions,
and pieces for social media.

## Questions of its own
- What is being launched? Is there video, images, a presentation? Brand and colors?
- Guests and press? Check-in? Streaming?
- Music, a countdown to the start?

## What to build
- Scenes with the brand identity (lightweight backgrounds, logo DSK); opening video as a cue.
- Countdown/standby slate (evento_preparar) with music.
- Presentation as slides; lower thirds for the speakers.

## Check
The video plays with audio; the presentation shows; the stream.

## Hand over
The event website and the stream link; the check-ins.
