# Local sports event

## What it's for
A match or tournament streamed with an on-screen scoreboard, game clock, play-by-play commentary,
replays and sponsor logos.

## Questions of its own
- Which sport, teams or participants? Team logos?
- Which cameras are available? Play-by-play announcer and commentator (remote)?
- Scoreboard and clock on screen? Replays?
- Sponsors? Streaming?

## What to build
- Scoreboard graphic with teams and score (grafico_mostrar with its values) and a clock.
- Scenes: “Field”, “Commentators”, “Replay” (replay_ajustes / replay_guardar), “Halftime”.
- Sponsor logos in rotation (DSK or carousel).

## Check
Scoreboard updating; replay saving; commentary audio over the ambient sound.

## Hand over
The stream link; the control surface for the scoreboard if someone else runs it.
