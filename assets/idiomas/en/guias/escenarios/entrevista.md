# Interview or podcast

## What it's for
A conversation recorded or live: audio comes first. Afterwards it gets cut down for social media.

## Questions of its own
- How many hosts and guests? Is anyone remote?
- Live or recorded? For which platform (vertical for social media)?
- Music bed, intro, bumpers?
- Separate recording per person for editing later? (grabacion_individual)

## What to build
- Scenes: “Two-shot”, “Host”, “Guest”, “Remote”, “Intro”/“Closing”.
- Audio: each microphone with its rack (compressor, limiter), program loudness (audio_loudness_objetivo).
- Lower thirds with the guest's name and the topic.

## Check
Levels of each microphone (audio_medidores), echo from the remote guest (invitado_prueba).

## Hand over
The remote guest's link; at the end, the recording (and the individual tracks).
