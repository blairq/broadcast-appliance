The chamber's conference system (Bosch DCN Next Generation) is handled by the `bosch-dcn` plugin; these tools talk about **seats** (those of the Body), not units.
1. **Connect:** `dcn_puertos` (the USB-serial adapters) and `dcn_conectar {"transporte": "serie", "puerto": ..., "velocidad": 19200}`; over the network (CCU2) `{"transporte": "tcp", "host": ...}`; to test without the equipment `{"transporte": "simulador", "solo_escuchar": false}`. By default it stays in **listen only** (it receives everything, sends no commands): turn it off with `dcn_conectar {"solo_escuchar": false}` once the room responds well.
2. **Units:** each seat needs its unit (`dcn_unidad` in the Body). `dcn_unidades` tells which are missing; `dcn_unidad_asignar {"aprender_todas": true}` and someone presses the button of each seat in the room, in order.
3. **Operate:** `dcn_estado` (open, requests in order, `siguiente`), `dcn_microfono {"banca", "abierto"}`, `dcn_siguiente`, `dcn_pedido {"banca", "accion"}`, `dcn_cerrar_todos` (doesn't touch the chair), `dcn_modo {"modo": "operador", "max_abiertos": 4}`.
- The app and the control surface give the floor only in **operator** mode; in the other modes the conference system decides and you still see what happens.
- `banca_al_aire` with the conference system connected also opens that seat's microphone (and closes the previous one); `recinto_orador_terminar` closes it.
- The tools that open or close microphones are **on-air** tools: if something automatic triggers them while streaming, they ask for confirmation.
- Each microphone notice goes out on the bus as `plugin/bosch-dcn/conferencia/microfono {banca, abierto}`: Follow the microphone (source “conferencia”) uses it.
- **Voting:** `dcn_votacion {"accion": "preparar", "tema": ..., "menu": "si_no_abstencion"}`, then `abrir` and `cerrar`; `dcn_votacion_resultado` (partials and roll-call by seat); the official one goes to Quorum with `dcn_votacion_a_quorum` (never load it without a person asking for it).
- **Attendance:** `dcn_asistencia`; `dcn_presentes_quorum {"activo": true}` makes Quorum count those present.
- **Timers:** with an in-room speaker on air with an assigned time, their unit's clock sets itself and warns at one minute and at the end (`dcn_tiempo` to do it by hand).
- **Dual DDI:** a DCN-DDI in dual mode is two microphones: the left half is unit N and the right one N+256; each one is assigned to its seat (docs/bosch/dcn-ddi.md).
- **Undocumented functions** (from Bosch's software, untested against a real CCU): `dcn_funciones` lists them and `dcn_funcion {"nombre", "args"}` calls them; the dangerous ones ask for `confirmar`. With their own name: `dcn_proximo` (next to speak, roll-call turn), `dcn_cerrar_tras_siguiente {"activo": true}`, `dcn_llamado_nominal {"bancas": [...]}`. If the chair runs the vote from their unit, the state of `dcn_votacion_resultado` follows it on its own.
- Problems: `dcn_traza {"activa": true}` and `dcn_traza {"ultimas": 50}` (frames in hexadecimal and retransmission counters). Full guide: docs/bosch/open-interface.md.
