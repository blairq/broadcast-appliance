The virtual seat (docs/funciones/SESION-REMOTA.md): each member of the Body can join from the browser (phone, tablet, computer) with their **personal link and PIN**, whether they are in the chamber or at home. It is also the backup for the conference system: if the CCU goes down, the members join with their phones.
1. **Link and PIN:** `sesion_credencial {"banca": "12"}` generates them and shows them ONCE (if there was one already, the previous one stops working). With `sesion_acceso_opciones {"acceso_fijo": true}` they stay fixed: they can be asked for again and it returns the same ones, and they only change with `sesion_credencial {"banca", "renovar": true}`; with `{"pin_en_qr": true}` the PIN goes inside the QR (and in `link_con_pin`): scan and join without typing. They are given to the member through a secure channel. `sesion_clave {"banca", "usuario"}` gives them a username and password to log in by typing, without the link; `sesion_datos` stores their email, phone and a note. `sesion_revocar` cuts them off. All of this is also in Assembly → Remote connections.
2. **Enable:** when they join they are “waiting”. A person looks at them through the camera (in Assembly → Chamber their seat shows their face and blinks amber) and enables them: `sesion_habilitar {"banca": "12"}`. Once enabled, they count as present in Quorum (without counting them twice if the conference system already has them present). **Never enable someone no person has seen.**
3. `sesion_estado`: who is connected, from where (room/outside), who enabled them. `sesion_expulsar` removes them.
4. `sesion_registro {"texto": true}`: what goes into the minutes (joins, enablements, failed attempts, lockouts).
- Security: 5 wrong PINs lock that access for 15 minutes; one member, one connection.
- **The floor:** the phone requests the floor and joins the same queue (`banca_cola`); `banca_al_aire` gives them the floor with their countdown on the phone; `banca_tiempo` adds or removes time.
- **Voting:** if the conference system opens a vote, the enabled members get the red banner on their phone; without the conference system, `sesion_votacion {"accion": "abrir", "tema", "menu", "segundos"}`. One vote per member goes to Quorum.
- **Chair's console:** `sesion_consola {"accion": "crear"}` gives the link and PIN for …/presidencia/ (chamber with faces, queue and seats with “press to speak”, mute everyone, “I'll speak”, debate, voting, live program).
- **Their image and voice:** whoever has the floor comes in as “Remoto con la palabra” (audio, video and “— contenido”, what they share); in a debate between remote members the other comes in as “Remoto con la palabra 2”. The scenes have to show those inputs.
- **What they see on the phone:** their camera, the main output or another scene (`sesion_vistas`), with their own image in a box.
- **Talking to them:** `sesion_hablar {"banca", "activo"}` (your room microphone reaches their phone).
