Check-in by kiosk (docs/funciones/REGISTRO-TOTEM.md): a web page (`/registro/`) on a TV with a camera and microphone, a tablet or the phone of whoever scans its QR. The person shows their face, the assistant greets them by voice (“Hi Ana, welcome to…”), asks them out loud for the configured fields and answers by speaking. What they answer goes into the **open event's roll** (`datos` of each attendee, `porDonde: "registro"`) and they're checked in; if the face wasn't known, it's added to People with its photo (and the name if they said it).
1. **Set up:** `totem_crear {"nombre"}` (by default it asks for name and email). `totem_cambiar {"id", "campos": [...]}` replaces the list of fields: `{clave, etiqueta, pregunta, tipo, obligatorio, opciones?, ejemplo?}` with type `texto | nombre | correo | telefono | numero | sino | opcion`. The greeting, the farewell and “already checked in” accept {hola}, {nombre} and {evento}. `confirmar` repeats each field back and asks “is that right?”; `reconocer` greets whoever it recognizes by name (and doesn't ask for their name). They belong to THAT event (Eventos/<x>/registro.json).
2. **Bring it to people:** `totem_enlace {"id", "mostrar": true}` (url and QR; the url carries a secret: don't post it just anywhere), `totem_proyectar {"id", "pantalla"}` to a TV (it shows the QR large for scanning), or the screen catalog (“registro:<id>”).
3. **Watch:** `totem_estado` (who's checking in, at which step, what was understood), `totem_registrados` (what they answered). Up to 8 conversations at once (several kiosks at different doors).
- **The ID card through the camera:** a field `{"tipo": "documento", "documentos": ["dni-ar"]}` asks them “Show your ID to the camera, barcode side”, reads the PDF417, confirms it (“You're Juana Prueba, DNI 11.222.333, is that right?”) and fills in the name (and the apellido/nombres/dni/nacimiento/cuil keys that have no data); the whole document stays in `datos[<clave>]`. If it can't read it in 15 s it tells them what to correct and, on the second try, if it isn't required, it skips it; the number can be typed or dictated. **The back side:** if there's a model with vision (`documento_tipos` → `dorso`), when the front's code doesn't come through it asks “Turn it over and show me the back, the side with the lines of letters and numbers at the bottom”: a vision model transcribes the machine-readable zone from the bottom strip of the camera's full photos —first the local model (download the “Vision for the 4B models” add-on with `modelo_local_bajar`; the photo doesn't leave the machine), otherwise your own model (the photo goes only to its server)— and it's accepted if the check digits of the number, the birth date and the expiry date match and, in addition, the composite one or another photo that agrees (a model can make things up); it's confirmed the same way as the front. Without vision the back isn't requested. Other types (a driver's license, another country's ID) come in through a plugin: `documento_tipos` lists them (with `mrz-td1`, any ID card with an MRZ zone), `documento_leer {"video": "registro:<canal>"}` tests the camera (`"lado": "dorso"` tests the back side). Through MCP, documents come out summarized (name and last three digits): the complete data doesn't leave the machine.
4. **Step in:** `totem_atender {"conn", "accion": "atender"}` pauses that conversation's script (the assistant goes quiet; the kiosk says “You're being helped by…”), `totem_dato {"conn", "clave", "valor"}` fills in or corrects a field (whether attended or not; empty deletes it) and `totem_atender {"conn", "accion": "guardar" | "devolver" | "cancelar"}` finishes. For staff who help from their phone or computer (they see all the conversations, watch and listen to one, talk to that kiosk): `totem_atencion_enlace {"mostrar": true}` (link and QR; it carries a secret).
- **The model that understands** (`conModelo`, on by default): the script still asks for one field at a time, but what the person says is understood by the “registro” role's model (Agents tab; by default the local model, which doesn't send anything off the machine): if they say everything at once (“I'm John Smith, my email is…”) it records each field through tools that validate it and the script confirms them together; it understands corrections (“no, the email is with a J”) and answers short questions about the event with the kiosk's `ficha` (schedule, venue, rooms); for anything else it says it can't help and the script resumes. If the model doesn't answer in time, the script carries on alone without losing what was recorded. `totem_estado` says what it's understanding with (`agente`, and `modo` per connection).
- Write the questions the way they're spoken: short, one at a time, in the event's language (“Can you tell me your phone number?”).
- To speak it needs the speech recognizer and the attendant's voice (the same ones as spoken assistance: `atencion_estado`). Without voice, the page shows the text and you can type.
- In the interface: People → Check-in (`ui_show_page {"page": "personas", "seccion": "registro"}`).
